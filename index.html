See formulas & assumptions

Monthly rate r = annual rate ÷ 1200. EMI = P × r ÷ [1 − (1 + r)−n]. At 0% interest, EMI = P ÷ n. Total interest = EMI × n − P.

Break-even units = ceiling(fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost)). No break-even is reported when contribution per unit is zero or negative. Operating profit = units × (price − variable cost) − fixed costs. Cash after EMI = operating profit − EMI.

Household buffer = existing household income − expenses − existing EMIs − proposed EMI; projected business profit is not added. No lending eligibility decision is made. Inputs are monthly except annual interest. Taxes, fees, depreciation, working-capital changes and insurance are excluded. Results are estimates, rounded only for display.

Method references: Axis Bank EMI · SBA break-even
